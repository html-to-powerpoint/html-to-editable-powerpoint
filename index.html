Supported elements remain editable where possible. Complex effects may be rasterized; test your own HTML before integrating.

Generate the same slide

  1. Get a key and plan from the RapidAPI listing.
  2. Download request.json and follow the cURL example.
  3. Save the binary response as a file and open it in PowerPoint.

Set type to pptx, pdf, png or jpeg. Submit 1–20 slides for PPTX/PDF, or one slide for PNG/JPEG.
